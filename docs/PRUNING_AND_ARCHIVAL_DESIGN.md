# Pruning and Archival Protocol

This repository does not define an independent pruning and archival protocol.

The sole source of truth is the [DIDzM Pruning and Archival Protocol](../../DIDzMonolith-docs/standards/DIDZM_PRUNING_AND_ARCHIVAL_PROTOCOL.md).

## RWAz responsibility

RWAz defines asset-specific lifecycle facts and identifies which asset records are authoritative, derived, operational, legally held, or eligible for pruning.

RWAz does not operate archival storage or redefine the root protocol. Asset ownership, custody, retirement, destruction, succession, disputes, and legal holds remain distinct lifecycle concepts.
